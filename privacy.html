Pocket Cards · Privacy

Privacy notice

Updated 25 October 2026. This notice describes Pocket Cards through version 0.2.0.

What the app stores

Pocket Cards saves the card names, barcode values and formats, notes and colours that you enter. These are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. A separate local guest wallet is available without connecting an account.

Optional Google synchronization

If you connect Google, the app uses your account email to keep your wallet separate from other accounts. It stores card changes and deletion markers in that account’s hidden Google Drive app-data folder so your phones can synchronize. It requests access to its own app-data storage and your account email. It does not request access to your ordinary Drive documents.

Google handles authorization. Pocket Cards does not collect your Google password. Access tokens are held in app memory rather than written to the wallet files. Google processes account and service data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app’s Google access through your Google account settings.

Pictures and sharing

Selected barcode pictures are decoded on your phone. Camera scanning is provided by Google Play services. Pocket Cards does not upload selected pictures to a developer server. Google Play services may process service and diagnostic data under Google’s policies.

Optional Klarna import

If you choose Import cards from Klarna, an embedded browser opens Klarna’s official website. You sign in directly with Klarna. The importer does not read login fields, passwords, authentication tokens or session-cookie values. After you start importing, it reads the loyalty-card names, displayed numbers and original barcode images from Klarna’s wallet pages and decodes them on your phone. You review the result before cards are added to your current wallet.

The importer clears its browser cookies, cache and web storage when you leave or collection finishes. Confirmed cards are saved like other wallet cards and can sync to your Google account if connected. Klarna processes your sign-in and browsing under its own privacy notice. This optional importer depends on Klarna’s website and embedded-browser support; it is not an official Klarna integration.

When you choose Share, the app creates a PNG in its temporary cache and grants the receiving app access to that image. A later cleanup removes cached exports more than a day old. The recipient and receiving app control any copies they keep; deleting a card cannot recall a picture you already shared.

Deleting cards and account data

Deleting a card removes it from wallet views after your phones synchronize. Earlier values can remain in the synchronization history in Google Drive’s app-data storage. To remove that cloud history, use Google Drive’s app-management controls to delete this app’s hidden data. Other phones can retain local copies until you remove them.

Signing out removes that account’s wallet from the phone after its pending changes have synchronized. It does not delete the cloud history or the separate guest wallet. Uninstalling the app removes its local app storage, but does not remove pictures you shared or the Google Drive app-data history. Device backup and transfer of the app’s wallet files are disabled.

Other services

Pocket Cards has no developer-run storage server, advertising service or application analytics service. Google authorization and synchronization use HTTPS. These information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub’s hosting practices.

Contact

For questions about the app or this notice, contact the developer on GitHub.
