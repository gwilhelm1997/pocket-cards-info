Pocket Cards · Privacy

Your loyalty cards, ready when you need them.

Pocket Cards is an Android wallet for family use. Add a card by typing its number, scanning it, or choosing a picture. Open the card to show its barcode, or share a barcode picture using your phone’s share menu.

Your account, your wallet

Connect your Google account to sync cards through your private Google Drive app-data storage. Connect the same account on another phone to restore them. Each account has a separate wallet.

Local cards work offline. Google connection and synchronization require internet access and a supported Google account. Android 10 or newer is required.

Availability

The Android APK is distributed privately by the owner. These public pages provide app information and its privacy notice. They do not contain card data or account credentials.

Read the privacy notice or contact the developer on GitHub.
